HOW TO PLAY

SURVIVE THE RIOT

Waves of stickmen keep coming. Clear a wave to heal 16 HP and bank a bonus. Every fifth wave, the boss walks in.

MOVES

THREE-HIT COMBO
Punch three times fast. The third punch is an uppercut that launches.
KICK
Sends them flying. A flying body knocks down anyone it hits: that's a STRIKE.
JUGGLE
Hit enemies while they're in the air for bonus points.
RIOT SUPER
Every hit fills the RIOT meter. When it's full, unleash it to blast the whole ring off its feet.
MULTIPLIER
Every 4 hits in a row raises it, up to x10. Getting hit resets it.

ENEMIES

GRUNT
The basic brawler. A ! over its head means a swing is coming.
RUNNER
From wave 2. Fast and fragile. Jabs, then backs off.
BRUTE
From wave 3. Huge and slow. When you see the !, back off or jump.
SHIELD
From wave 4. Blocks punches from the front. Kick to break the guard, or hit it from behind.
THE BOUNCER
Every fifth wave. Too heavy to launch, and it pounds the floor. Jump the shockwaves.

THE RING

LIVE ROPES
Launch enemies into the electric ropes on either side for a ZAP bonus. Get knocked into them yourself and you get zapped too.
